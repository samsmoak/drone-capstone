1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Samuel, 2026-10-24: "we first detect the two base stations, then we draw that
room on the spot, and we show the drone's position in that particular room …
forward, backwards, left and right on the drone, so that the user can easily
see that before they press start a flight and as they are flying it."

Manual's console, Vitals tab, top to bottom: the attitude indicator, the ROOM,
the command log. The vitals tail is gone from Manual (the strip on the right
carries the vitals) and so is the Scene tab — deleted outright, SceneView
with it (Samuel: "it's of no use"). The map FILLS its pane: drag the dividers
up and down between the attitude indicator, the room and the log, or sideways
between the console and the controls, and the map grows with them
(RoomMap `fill`). The room is Auto's own map (auto/plan/RoomMap.tsx) — 2-D or 3-D,
the tripods with their channels, the origin and Forward — with nothing drawn
by hand:

  the room    the green: where the measured stations' light reaches over the
              agent's flying area, 0.1 m to the ceiling (the same prediction
              as Auto's ③, coverage.py predict, with no room)
  the drone   live, 2.5x Auto's size, with its trail since the window opened
              (green where measured, dashed amber where only estimated)
  the keys    F B L R round it — where ↑ ↓ ← → move it NOW; the nose is a
              separate red tick (bead in 3-D)
  no position the stations not heard: the drone cannot be placed, so a compass
              with ↑ at the top and the nose turning under it

The right strip shows "Base stations 2 of 2 · Ch 1 Ch 2" (amber with one, red
with none); the command log writes one line each time that changes.

3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• THE KEYS ARE DRAWN WHERE THEY MOVE IT, NOT ON THE NOSE. The arrows never
  follow the nose (keyframe.py, locked 2026-09-30): ↑ is away from the
  operator, the room's forward, or the takeoff heading. An F on the nose would
  lie after the first A/D turn. lib/keyDirections.ts mirrors resolve() — the
  agent sends `controls.live` only when the MEANING changes, so "away from
  you" is recomputed here from the spot and the drone each frame, with the
  same 0.50/0.60 m hysteresis.
• SHOWN, NOT ENFORCED. The green is where the position can be trusted, not a
  fence; the guard still holds the agent's square (session._fence). Making the
  manual guard stop at the green changes the safety rules — left for Samuel.
• NO ROOM PICKER. An earlier plan had one; Samuel: "we are not drawing any
  room … just using the auto detection". The room is the stations' reach.
• STATIONS NOT MEASURED → MEASURE HERE. Manual has no ② Position step, so the
  map offers the same stages and Measure button (StationStages) itself.
• NOT IN THE LOCK. Nothing in manual.py, keyframe.py, tuning.py, keys.ts,
  arrows.ts, ArrowFrame.tsx or App.tsx's keyboard handler changed;
  test_manual_control_lock.py passes untouched.
• BAROMETER FLIGHT IS THE SECOND BUTTON. Unassisted at confirmation, the
  agent's reason is shown first, "Look again for the base stations" (retry) is
  primary and "Fly on the barometer — I am flying it by eye" secondary.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• "HEARD" IS OVER A WINDOW, NEVER ONE SAMPLE. bsReceive flickers per sample
  with both stations in view — the 2026-10-24 Auto traces read 0, 1, 2, 3 in
  turn. A station counts as heard if any sample in the last 2 s had its bit;
  one sample would log "lost" ten times a second.
• Telemetry `at` is SECONDS (session.py time.time()), not milliseconds.
• WHY THIS EXISTS: the 2026-10-24 05:38 manual flight heard no station (all
  four light sensors 0 for the session), flew on the barometer, and bounced —
  the barometer swung -0.6 to +2.5 m against a 0.62 m target, thrust slammed
  20 000 to 65 535. Nothing on screen had said the stations were not heard;
  the Auto session three minutes earlier heard both. The reason is now kept in
  meta.json and said before barometer flight.
