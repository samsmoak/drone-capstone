1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two things the operator controls about the camera in a session:

• AUTO-CAPTURE, a switch: on, the camera's frames are recorded on their own;
  off, the camera stays live and nothing is stored unless captured by hand
  (Samuel, 2026-10-24: "when off it doesnt mean the camera goes black").
• CAPTURE, a button: the picture on screen, saved into the session, labelled
  "Captured manually", always uploaded — on the Capture page, which starts and
  ends its own session so a quick test needs nothing else ("they can just
  start a session in the manual capture image page and capture the images and
  end the session there and move out").

And, with auto-capture on, which frames count as CAPTURED — uploaded and
shown — instead of hundreds of copies of the same view (the rule, §3).

3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• THE RULE (Samuel, 2026-10-24: "if there are 3 same images captured from the
  past three seconds … if they do not differ from the ones that we already
  have, don't save them"). With auto-capture on:
      first frame               captured
      within 0.5 s of the last  never — 2 a second at most, the upload pacing
                                decided 2026-09-24
      a point is being held     captured
      changed (≥ 2.0)           captured; the run of similar ones restarts
      similar                   once a second, the first 3 in a row only
      still for 30 s            one anyway (Samuel: "okay thats fine, if the
                                auto capture is on")
  Compared with the LAST CAPTURED frame, never the previous one — a slow
  drift is a series of small steps and would never be captured otherwise.
• EVERY FRAME STAYS ON DISK; "captured" decides what uploads and is shown. A
  flight cannot be re-run (CLAUDE.md #6), and the pipeline's scene@1 and the
  backlog scrubber read them all. Off is the exception: nothing is written.
• MANUAL CAPTURES ARE NEVER PACED and upload whatever the switch says.
• A SESSION IS REQUIRED to capture. Its recording opens when the checks pass,
  before the area is confirmed — confirming is for flying, so the Capture page
  does not ask for it.
• ONE SESSION, TWO PAGES. The Capture page and Control drive the same agent
  session: started on either, joined on the other. "Capture images →" on
  Control during a session, "Fly it on Control →" on Capture.
• CAPTURE SITS IN THE CAMERA PANEL'S HEADER: measured, it fell below the fold
  when placed under the picture at 1280 px.
• THE SWITCH IS ON BY DEFAULT and per session, like the DPP's (the choice
  resets when the session ends), but it applies AT ONCE — the recording reads
  it on every frame; the DPP's is read as a flight begins.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• "THE SAME" MEANS UNDER CHANGE_THRESHOLD, NOT EQUAL. Measured 2026-10-24 on
  the lab laptop: not one pair of consecutive frames was byte-identical, yet
  in 9 of 11 sessions over 80 % were near-copies. Mean absolute difference on
  a 4x-downsampled copy: a still scene p50 0.35, p90 0.42, the highest of any
  still session 1.7; the drone moving 2 to 50. Hence 2.0.
• A FRAME THE DISK REFUSES NEVER BECOMES THE REFERENCE (decide, then commit
  after the write) — or the next real change would compare against a picture
  that was never saved.
• AN UNDECODABLE FRAME FALLS BACK TO THE PACING, captured as "undecoded": a
  capture is never lost to a decoding problem.
• frames.csv COLUMNS ARE ONLY EVER APPENDED. Readers go by name (the pipeline,
  the syncer, the web); a row from before `source` existed reads as "auto".
• A CAPTURE NEEDS A FRESH FRAME: the camera's frame() is None once it is older
  than 2 s, and the route refuses in words — a stalled feed's last picture is
  not what the operator is looking at.
• NO KEYBOARD SHORTCUT. App.tsx's flight keys listen on every page, and they
  are locked; L still lands from the Capture page.
