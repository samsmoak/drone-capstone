1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pick a flight in Sessions and watch it again: the room, the plan it flew (after
auto-correct — the plan AS FLOWN), the path the drone really took drawn behind
it, and the drone where it was at the moment on the slider, with its height,
speed, drift, battery, temperature, pressure, heading, the loop's state, the
keys the operator held, and the point it was holding. The events say what
happened in words — took off, holding at P1, base stations lost for 4.7 s,
operator held ArrowUp, no samples for 1.5 s, landing — and each one jumps the
slider to its moment. A Manual flight plays back too, over the default area
with no path.

IT IS A RECORDING, NOT A SIMULATION. A simulator flying the same plan shows an
ideal drone; the failures worth replaying come from the room (reception,
drift, a guard, an operator), and only the recording has them. The simulator's
job is CI (../development/releases.txt, agent-tests.yml).

3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• IN SESSIONS, NOT A NEW PAGE. Sessions already lists every flight; a Play back
  button on each is where people already look, and the player replaces the
  session's page with Back to return (2026-10-11, Samuel agreed).
• THE MISSION REPORT'S CLOCK. Every time is seconds from the earlier of the two
  files' first rows — mission/review.py load()'s origin — so a time on the
  slider and a time in the report are the same time.
• THE PATH FLOWN IS RUNS (trail.ts). A gap in the samples ends a run (no line
  across time nobody saw); a change between measured and unmeasured position
  starts a new one in the other style.
• IN A GAP THE DRONE IS NOT DRAWN. Nothing was received, so nothing is shown —
  the drone "disappears", which is what happened on the link.
• ONE EVENT PER STRETCH WITHOUT BASE STATIONS, with its length. On the lab's
  flights reception flickers (lost and back within 0.2 s, several times a
  flight); a line per flicker buried the stretch that mattered — d985c7a6 held
  P1 for 4.7 s with no base station.
• ONE MAP AT A TIME. Full screen replaces the inline map: two WebGL views of one
  room are two GPU contexts.
• Capped at an hour per file (playback.MAX_ROWS, 36 000 rows), and it says so.
  The lab's missions are 15–42 s, 60–165 KB.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• NO KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS IN PLAYBACK. App.tsx's flight keys listen on the whole
  window on every page: Space is swallowed (it must never press a button that
  stops the motors), L LANDS THE DRONE, the arrows and W A S D fly it in
  Manual. Playback is buttons, a <select> and the native slider, whose own
  arrow keys never reach the flight handler (it ignores keys typed into an
  input or select). A "Space to play" here would be a key that lands a drone
  on another page's terms. (App.tsx and keys.ts are LOCKED — CLAUDE.md.)
• The flight CSV's x_m, y_m are the room's frame — the frame the plan is drawn
  in; z_m is height above the floor at takeoff (telemetry/reader.py), what the
  map's drone takes. Never draw stateEstimate.z: Lighthouse z = 0 is not the
  floor (CLAUDE.md invariant 4).
• positioned is lighthouse_received > 0. False means the drone flew on its own
  estimate — drawn, but dashed amber and said.
• The unmeasured stretch is amber on a --foreground casing: amber alone is
  1.7:1 on the light floor, under WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 (measured 2026-10-11);
  green is 3.1:1 and needs none.
• Full screen, stacked (below lg): no min-h-0 on the columns. With it the grid
  rows shrank below their content and the readings slid over the map at
  720 px — the DOM measurement passed it; only the screenshot showed it.
• A flight id comes from a URL: anything but [A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,64} is a 404
  before it touches the disk (processing.FLIGHT_ID).
