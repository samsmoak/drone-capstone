1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pick any flight — yours or a teammate's — and watch it again: the room, the
plan it flew (after auto-correct: the plan AS FLOWN), the path the drone really
took drawn behind it, and the drone where it was at the moment on the slider,
with its height, speed, drift, battery, temperature, pressure, heading, the
loop's state, the keys the operator held, and the point it was holding. The
events say what happened in words — took off, holding at P1, base stations
lost for 4.7 s, operator held ArrowUp, no samples for 1.5 s, landing — and
each jumps the slider to its moment. A Manual flight plays back too, over the
default area with no path.

IT IS A RECORDING, NOT A SIMULATION. The failures worth replaying come from
the room (reception, drift, a guard, an operator), and only the recording has
them. The simulator's job is CI (../development/releases.txt, agent-tests.yml).

3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• PER FLIGHT, THIS LAPTOP FIRST, SUPABASE FOR THE REST (2026-10-11, Samuel).
  A flight with files here plays from them — instant, complete, offline, and
  the moment it lands; any other plays from Supabase: a teammate's, and yours
  from another computer or after the files here were deleted. Never "wait
  until it is uploaded". The list shows each flight once, with where it is:
  "On this computer · uploading / uploaded" or "In Supabase".
• ONE BUILDER FOR BOTH SOURCES (playback._build). A flight looks the same
  from either copy; test_playback_cloud.py and the real-flight check prove it
  field for field — samples, control, gaps, events, report, plan, room.
• UPLOAD, NOT A NEW PIPELINE: the plan and trace ride the existing outbox, as
  session vitals do (copied from sync/samples.py): written first, sent after,
  a cursor, last in the pass. Past flights are queued once when the updated
  agent starts, so a teammate's old flights appear without a command.
• ITS OWN PAGE, IN THE SIDEBAR (2026-10-11, Samuel). Playback first shipped
  inside Sessions (#108, #109: a Play back button, then an "All flights"
  switch) on my recommendation; Samuel had asked for a page of its own with
  a sidebar link, and it is that now. The list lives on the Playback page
  only; a session's flights keep a Play back button into the same player.
• THE MISSION REPORT'S CLOCK. Every time is seconds from the earlier of the
  flight's first sample and first trace row — review.load's origin — so the
  slider and the report agree. The report is computed from the same rows.
• THE PATH FLOWN IS RUNS (trail.ts): a gap ends a run; a change between
  measured and unmeasured position starts one in the other style. In a gap the
  drone is not drawn — nothing was received.
• ONE EVENT PER STRETCH WITHOUT BASE STATIONS, with its length: reception
  flickers on the lab's flights; d985c7a6 held P1 for 4.7 s with none.
• Capped at an hour (playback.MAX_ROWS); the list at the newest 300.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• NO KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS, AND NO TABS, ON PLAYBACK. App.tsx's flight keys listen on the
  whole window on every page: Space swallowed, L LANDS, the arrows and W A S D
  fly it in Manual. Playback is buttons, <select>s, a checkbox and the native
  slider, whose keys never reach the flight handler (it ignores inputs and
  selects). components/Tabs.tsx moves on the arrows WITHOUT stopping them — a
  focused tab's arrow also flies the drone; see GRAPH_STATE.txt WATCH.
• HEIGHT ERROR IS COMPUTED FROM THE TWO READINGS, from either source. A float8
  column comes back from PostgREST with 15 significant digits; on fdd57690 the
  report's hold-height figures differed in the 15th digit until it was read
  from the trace row's jsonb `values`, which keeps every digit.
• PostgREST answers at most 1 000 rows a request: every read pages
  (cloud.PAGE). Proven on 2 500-row telemetry and trace.
• Append-only in Supabase: a plan or trace row is never edited; a re-send is
  ignored. A plan for a flight another account flew is refused BY THE POLICY
  and the upload gives the record up rather than retrying it every pass.
• The flight CSV's x_m, y_m are the room's frame; z_m is height above the
  takeoff floor. Never draw stateEstimate.z (CLAUDE.md invariant 4).
• The unmeasured stretch is amber on a --foreground casing: amber alone is
  1.7:1 on the light floor, under WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1; green is 3.1:1.
• Lay the list out by its own width (@container): four columns squeezed an
  email to "gmail.c / om" at 720 px. Stacked full screen: no min-h-0 on the
  columns — the readings slid over the map; only a screenshot showed it.
• A flight id comes from a URL: anything but [A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,64} is a 404
  before it touches the disk (processing.FLIGHT_ID). Every route is
  token-gated: they name people.
