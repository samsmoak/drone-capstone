--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In Auto the Control page is for missions. The left column is a five-step flow,
adapted from the Zoomaa booking flow: ① Plan — pick a saved mission, view it,
edit its room and path, or plan a new one; ② Position — the drone learns where
it is, one press from where it sits; ③ Flyable space — the green, predicted
from the base station or walked; ④ Auto-correct — a copy of the plan fitted
into the green beside the plan as drawn, which is never changed; ⑤ Fly — start
the session, the checks, confirm the area, then Start mission, the mission on
the room map, the points ticking off, and each point's verdict once it lands. The whole left
section can go full screen. The right column is the monitor: the vitals, keys
and actions (FlightDeck), then the Vitals and Camera tabs. Manual is unchanged
except for the shared fixes in pages-and-windows.txt and its own DPP panel.

3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• THREE STEPS, NOT TWO. Each step's completion is READ FROM THE AGENT (a valid
  mission; state "ready" with no retry owed) — the page cannot claim a step the
  agent would refuse.
• THE FLOW LOCKS ON FLY WHILE A MISSION FLIES: no plan changes in the air.
• THE VITALS AND KEYS STAY ABOVE THE TABS (FlightDeck), and the action rail
  keeps its place in both modes — pages-and-windows.txt: they "must not be
  behind a tab or below a fold".
• SIDE BY SIDE FROM 1280 px, STACKED BELOW (2026-09-29; it was 1024). At a
  1024 px window the monitor was 294 px wide: its tab list got 97 px for
  177 px of tabs and Camera ran into "10 Hz". The divider's own position
  (cropwatcher.split.control.auto, 58/42) now stops at 45–70 %.
• FULL SCREEN IS THE SAME FLOW, RESTYLED IN PLACE (AutoControl
  FullScreenFrame), not a copy in an overlay: the same elements with other
  classes, so the step, the chosen mission and an unsaved plan survive going in
  and out. Escape leaves it (not while a menu or a field has it). It covers the
  flight strip, so it carries LAND and the strip's own HOLD-TO-STOP emergency
  stop (exported from FlightStrip, not copied) whenever the drone is airborne.
• ONE ROOM MAP, TWO VIEWS (RoomMap). 2-D is the exact top-down plan; 3-D is the
  room's flying space, obstacles at their height, the path at its heights, the
  drone at its height and heading — orbit, pan, zoom, camera presets (3/4, Top,
  Front, Side, Fit), double-click to swing round one object. The choice is
  remembered (cropwatcher.auto.mapView) and V toggles it. Both views take the
  same editing callbacks, so every edit goes through the same clamping.
• THREE.JS, REVERSED ON PURPOSE. SceneView.tsx avoided it (~170 KB gz) and said
  to reach for it "if this ever needs an orbitable camera" — orbiting, picking
  and dragging are that. React Three Fiber 9 + drei 10 + three 0.186, loaded
  with React.lazy: a separate chunk (259 KB gz) fetched the first time 3-D is
  opened; the start-up bundle grew 218 → 226 KB gz for the new 2-D/editor code
  only (measured 2026-09-29 against main). The window's content policy is
  unchanged: labels are canvas sprites, not a downloaded font or drei <Html>
  (a React root per label, which React 19 warned about unmounting mid-render).
  Rendered on demand, never 60 fps beside the flight loop. No WebGL → 2-D with
  the reason said.
• THE LIST IS HOME (2026-09-29, the owner's ask). ① opens on the saved
  missions with New mission above them and Edit on every row; Edit opens the
  same editor a new mission uses. The editor is closed with Close or
  "← All missions" (both ask "Leave without saving?" when there are unsaved
  changes). Save returns to the list — not to the mission's view — with
  "Saved “name” (revision n)" and the saved row framed in --status-good, so the
  next thing (edit it again, plan another, pick one to fly) is one click. The
  message clears when the list is left.
• SELECTING ONE THING FADES THE REST — the room (its geofence) or one obstacle
  — whether it was clicked on the map (either view) or its card was clicked in
  the form. Its numbers appear UNDER the map (never over it: the panel covered
  the 3-D view's lower half until 2026-09-29). Its card is highlighted but is
  NOT scrolled into view: scrollIntoView scrolls every scrolling ancestor, and
  adding a point by clicking the map (the new point is selected as it is
  made) jumped the page away from the map to the Path list (the owner,
  2026-09-29). The numbers under the map make the scroll unnecessary.
  Points and the start select too, and fade nothing.
• NOTHING SELECTED IS A STATE (2026-09-29, the owner's ask): a click on empty
  floor (2-D or 3-D) or anywhere in the form outside a card selects nothing —
  every object shown equally, and NONE of them changeable. ONLY THE SELECTED
  OBJECT'S NUMBERS CHANGE: every other card shows its fields read-only
  (dashed); touching a card selects it, and that first click does only that —
  a checkbox does not toggle, Remove does not remove (ObjectCard). A field
  clicked or tabbed into selects its object and is editable at once.
• EACH OBJECT IS A CARD (ObjectCard): framed on all four sides, 1 px --muted;
  selected, 2 px --primary on all four sides (measured: 7.27 / 8.03 and
  6.07 / 3.95 : 1 on --surface-2, light / dark). The room, each obstacle, each
  point and the start are cards.
• A DIVIDER BETWEEN THE MAP AND THE FORM (cropwatcher.split.editor), inline
  and in full screen, from 600 px of EDITOR width (its own, measured — not the
  window's); stacked below that. Its bounds are pixels (map ≥ 300, form ≥
  280) turned into fractions of the live width, and SplitPane now clamps to
  bounds that move. Measured in the harness: a divider at 720, 900, 1024 and
  1600 px windows; stacked at 1280, where the app shows the monitor beside the
  flow and the editor is 585 px — drag the page's own divider to widen it.
• THE 3-D ROOM IS A DOLLHOUSE: its walls face inward and are drawn one-sided,
  so the walls between the camera and the room vanish and cannot be clicked.
  Double-sided, the near wall caught every click aimed at the floor inside the
  room and selected the room — empty floor could never select nothing. The far
  walls still select the room. The camera re-fits its preset to the view's
  new shape on a resize, unless the operator has turned it since.
• PLAIN WORDS, FIXED TO THE AXES (geometry.ts): LENGTH left→right, WIDTH
  front→back, HEIGHT up; positions are FROM LEFT and FROM FRONT of the room
  map's front-left corner, which never moves while shapes are edited. The owner
  found "left edge (x)", "bottom edge (y)", "depth" beside "width" confusing
  (2026-09-29). Only the labels changed: the data is absolute Lighthouse metres.
  A circle is set by its diameter. The room's top is its "Height"; its floor
  margin is "Lowest flying height".
• THE DRONE IS THE START. It is drawn by default as D (a green ring, the letter
  on --surface in --foreground); a new mission's planned start (S) is put where
  the drone is; "Put the start where the drone is" does it on demand. ② Check
  asks the agent for THE PLAN THAT WILL FLY every 2 s (GET /missions/{id}/
  from-drone; POST /missions/fit while an edit is unsaved) and ③ draws the
  path from it. Points never move with the drone — they mark equipment; only
  points OUTSIDE the flyable space are moved in, each named (floor-plans.txt).
• ② SHOWS TWO PLANS (2026-10-01, Samuel's ask). TOP: the plan as drawn over
  the flyable space (green: measured, or predicted with a dashed edge and a
  height slider in 2-D; a see-through volume with its outline at every height
  in 3-D); points outside it ringed red; "Edit the plan here" opens step ①'s
  own editor in place. BOTTOM: the plan that will fly, read-only, following
  every unsaved edit; each moved point listed with how far. Each map opens
  full screen. Under them, "Measure the flyable space" — the survey.
• THE END POINT: right-click a point (or its menu key / Shift+F10, or "End
  here" in its row, or the Ends-at list) and the flight lands there; later
  points stay in the plan, faded, "not flown". Making the LAST point the end is
  simply "not returning", so a point added later is still flown.
• SPEED BETWEEN POINTS: Steady / Normal / Brisk (10 / 15 / 20 cm/s), one per
  mission, under the start card (floor-plans.txt, ONE SPEED PER MISSION).
• DIRECTION IS THE ORDER. Every leg carries an arrow; "Reverse direction"
  reverses the order — with an end point set, the drone approaches it from the
  other side. No stored direction field.
• SAVE NEEDS A COMPLETE MISSION, NOT A SAFE ONE (2026-09-29, the owner's
  ask). Save stays grey until the ROOM is set up (a name, and an outline the
  agent accepts — 3+ corners, no crossing edges, inside the map) and the PATH
  is (a name, at least one point); a line under it says what is missing and
  jumps to that tab. Safety is not required: a half-finished plan with a
  problem is saved and fixed later — the agent's check gates "Use this
  mission" and Start instead. The agent refuses a pointless save too
  (store.save_mission, 422 in words).
• HEIGHTS HAVE ONE REFERENCE: the floor where the drone takes off (CLAUDE.md
  invariant 4). Every point is labelled with its height in both views, the
  Start section says so, and Auto's area confirmation reads "Drone on the
  floor and level…": set down on a bench — possible since the flight starts
  wherever the drone is — every point, and the ceiling guard's idea of the
  ceiling, would ride up by the bench's height, and no stored floor can catch
  it (the same room's floor read 0.85–1.40 m across sessions). Obstacle
  heights stay drawing-only for the same reason.
• THE AGENT CHECKS THE PLAN, 300 ms after each change (POST /missions/validate);
  its problems turn a point or a leg red. No copy of the rules in the window.
• Leaving an edited plan asks first, inline — not window.confirm, which a
  WebKit webview may not show.
• Start mission sits AT THE TOP of ③, disabled with its reason; the agent's own
  refusals (unsafe from the drone's position, battery, controller not built)
  arrive as the page message.
• THE DPP SWITCH is on ② (before the session) and ③ (beside Start): on by
  default in Auto (../pipeline/data-pipeline.txt). The RESULTS panel under
  Progress shows the last flight's verdicts, then its FINDINGS worst first
  (title, severity in a word, the agent's sentence, the camera note), and
  "Process again". A result from before 2026-10-09 has no findings and may
  still read "no classifier yet" — the panel says so. When a job finishes
  while the app is open with a warning or worse, the OS is notified once
  (desktop/src/lib/os-notify.ts; ../pipeline/interpret.txt).

4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Every async surface has its four states: the mission list; the room on Fly;
  the from-the-drone check (checking, no position yet — which now says to
  measure the base station in Set up step 3 — error with Try again,
  the answer); the results (processing, failed with the reason, not processed
  with Process this flight, the verdicts).
• Plan handles are real controls: focusable, named with where they are, moved
  5 cm per arrow key, focus visible (.plan-handle:focus-visible). The shape
  handle props must never carry `style` — spread after a shape's own style it
  replaced the fill (obstacles drew black; fixed 2026-09-28).
• 3-D drags always have a single-pointer alternative: the numbers in the form
  and the inspector (WCAG 2.2, 2.5.7). The camera presets are buttons.
• The point menu is a real menu: focus moves in, arrow keys move, Escape and a
  click elsewhere close it; it opens inward at the window's edge.
• The 3-D view's frame and every column around it are min-w-0 (and the frame
  overflow-hidden): a <canvas> is as wide as its pixels, and dragged narrower
  by the divider the view stayed full width and slid under the form.
• Nothing selected from the map may scroll the page: no scrollIntoView (or
  focus() on an off-screen element) in answer to a map click — the operator
  is looking at the map. It moved the page on every point added (2026-09-29).
• A SplitPane row must let its panes STRETCH: `items-start` collapsed the
  divider itself to 0 px tall, and nothing could grab it (2026-09-29).
• A <select> in the editor must be w-full min-w-0: a select is as wide as its
  longest option, and "P3 · Compressor manifold… — lands there" pushed the
  form past the window's right edge in full screen (harness, 2026-09-29).
• Full screen's key handler reads onExit through a ref and depends on `full`
  only: re-running it on the 10 Hz render pulled focus to Exit every frame.
• Measure with the harness before and after any change here: sections
  "Auto · ① Mission list", "① Mission editor", "② Check", "③ Fly, flying",
  "③ Fly, landed", "full screen editor" (?only=auto, ?map=2d|3d); the harness
  serves sample plans, a from-drone answer and a result when no agent is given,
  and keeps what is saved for the page's life (Save → the list can be driven:
  its Control page actions really run, against that fake).

• ② POSITION MEASURES FROM WHERE THE DRONE SITS — ONE PRESS (2026-10-05,
  Samuel: "no walking the drone back and forth"). Session.measure_station →
  geometry.estimate_quick: one 50-sweep sample, IPPE on the deck's four
  sensors, stored on the drone (it survives a power cycle), the estimator
  restarted from it; that spot becomes (0, 0, 0). IPPE's two answers are
  mirror images; the one whose STATION STANDS UPRIGHT wins (MIN_UPRIGHT
  0.5) — measured: (-1.33, +1.74, 1.49) up-z +0.89 against the mirror
  (+1.33, -1.74, 1.49) up-z -0.74. Neither upright is refused, never
  guessed; the two-record walk stays in the terminal (`cropwatcher
  geometry`) for that case. Done = the drone says positioning is ready
  (/station), never "a press happened". The camera watchdog never restarts
  the drone mid-measurement (Session.measuring_station).
• THE STEP BAR IS NEVER SQUEEZED (StepBar.tsx, 2026-10-05): full labels;
  wide, the steps share the width; narrow, it scrolls sideways.
• THE FLY MAP SHOWS THE AGENT'S AUTO-CORRECTED PLAN (GET …/from-drone every
  2 s before takeoff, kept while flying), not a copy worked out in the page;
  the page's own fromStart (whole path shifted) shows only until it arrives.
• THE SESSION STARTS IN ⑤ FLY, not its own step (Samuel). A session opened
  before ② sees no position until its checks run again: ② and ⑤ offer
  Check again (Session.retry, allowed while READY).
