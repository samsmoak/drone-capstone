1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In Auto the Control page is for missions. The left column is a three-step flow,
adapted from the Zoomaa booking flow: ① Mission — pick a saved mission, view
it, edit its room and path, or plan a new one; ② Check — start the session, run
the checks, confirm the area; ③ Fly — Start mission at the top, the mission
drawn in the scene, the points ticking off. The right column is the monitor:
the vitals, keys and actions (FlightDeck), then the Vitals and Camera tabs.
Manual is unchanged.

3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• THREE STEPS, NOT TWO. The session's checks lived in the right column; with
  the right column now the monitor they needed a home, and they are a sequence
  by nature. Each step's completion is READ FROM THE AGENT (a valid mission;
  state "ready" with no retry owed) — the page cannot claim a step the agent
  would refuse.
• THE FLOW LOCKS ON FLY WHILE A MISSION FLIES: no plan changes in the air.
• THE VITALS AND KEYS STAY ABOVE THE TABS (FlightDeck), and the action rail
  keeps its place in both modes — pages-and-windows.txt: they "must not be
  behind a tab or below a fold". The Scene moved into ③, where the mission it
  draws lives; the monitor's console shows Vitals and Camera.
• ITS OWN DIVIDER POSITION (cropwatcher.split.control.auto), starting 58/42 in
  the flow's favour: dragging in one mode must not move the other.
• EVERY SHAPE HAS ITS DIMENSIONS AS NUMBERS — the fence's width, depth or
  radius or each corner; each obstacle's centre and size or ends; each point's
  x, y, height, hold. Typing resizes the drawing and dragging updates the
  numbers. Every value is clamped to the ROOM'S MAP (measured coverage, or the
  default area) — nothing can be bigger than the space the drone can fly.
• THE AGENT CHECKS THE PLAN, 300 ms after each change (POST /missions/validate);
  its problems turn a point or a leg red. No copy of the rules in the window.
• The plan canvas is SVG whose units ARE metres — no pixel conversion to get
  wrong. No drawing library (the SceneView precedent: bundle size).
• Leaving an edited plan asks first, inline — not window.confirm, which a
  WebKit webview may not show.
• Start mission sits AT THE TOP of ③, disabled with its reason; the agent's own
  refusals (home, battery, controller not built) arrive as the page message.
• The Auto hover demo stays in the action rail until it becomes a one-point
  mission.

4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Every async surface has its four states: the mission list (skeleton, empty,
  error with Try again, content), the room on Fly (spinner, error with Try
  again, the scene).
• Plan handles are real controls: focusable, named with their position, moved
  5 cm per arrow key, focus visible (.plan-handle:focus-visible). The shape
  handle props must never carry `style` — spread after a shape's own style it
  replaced the fill (obstacles drew black; fixed 2026-09-28).
• Field (ControlPage.tsx) inputs are w-full: an input's intrinsic ~20
  characters ran under the Start button in ③ (harness, 2026-09-28).
• Measure with the harness before and after any change here: sections
  "Auto · ① Mission list", "① Mission editor", "② Check", "③ Fly, flying"
  (?only=auto); the harness serves sample plans when no agent is given.
