HOW AUTONOMOUS FLIGHT WORKS — the whole system, briefly
As of 2026-10-10, on main (built on experiment/samuel, 2026-10-05).
Detail for each part lives in the doc named in brackets.

================================================================================
THE ONE RULE EVERYTHING FOLLOWS
================================================================================
- Only the laptop with the Crazyradio can command the drone. The agent
  (Python, on that laptop) is the only thing that talks to the radio. The
  desktop app talks to the agent. [CLAUDE.md]

================================================================================
1. THE DRONE KNOWING WHERE IT IS  (light → angles → distance → position)
================================================================================
- Base station: sweeps laser lines across the room many times a second.
- Lighthouse deck (ON TOP of the drone): 4 light sensors. The drone times
  when each sweep hits each sensor → two ANGLES per sensor (a direction to
  the station). This decoding happens on the drone itself, not in our code.
- Every station needs its own CHANNEL (1, 2…), or its sweeps overlap and no
  angles decode. Set in Set up › 3 over the station's USB cable.
- Angles alone are directions, not distances. To get distance the drone needs
  the station's GEOMETRY: where it stands and which way it points.
- GEOMETRY (② Position, one press, drone where it sits): one sample of the 4
  sensors; their known 3 cm layout gives the station's distance (closer =
  the sensors look further apart). Two answers come out as mirror images;
  the one with the station standing UPRIGHT is kept. Stored on the drone
  (survives power-off). That spot becomes the origin (0, 0, 0).
- From then on the drone computes its own x, y, z many times a second
  (Kalman filter, "sweep angle" method — works with one station).
- A position counts only when the drone trusts it: a station in use AND its
  own uncertainty under 10 cm (under 5 cm to start a flight). Otherwise the
  app shows "no position" — never a drifting number.
  [desktop/setup.txt, desktop/auto-control.txt, backend/flight-safety.txt]

================================================================================
2. THE FLYABLE SPACE  (the green)
================================================================================
- Computed, not walked: from the stored geometry and the station's field of
  view (150° wide, 110° tall), over the whole map, in the room's height band.
- Green = where the drone can trust its position. It is real (tied to where
  the station is), unlike the drawn room.
- Shown on every map, including the editor; points outside it are ringed red.
  [missions/floor-plans.txt]

================================================================================
3. THE PLAN  (what you draw — ① Plan)
================================================================================
- ROOM: a closed fence + obstacles (optionally with a height to fly over).
  Drawn inside the agent's 4 m × 4 m area. Never limited by the green.
- MISSION: a planned start, inspection points (x, y, height, hold time), one
  speed (Steady 0.10 / Normal 0.15 / Brisk 0.20 m/s), return to start or an
  end point. Ceiling 1.0 m.
- Saved exactly as drawn. Nothing that happens at flight time changes it.
  [missions/floor-plans.txt]

================================================================================
4. THE PLAN THAT WILL FLY  (④ Auto-correct — automatic)
================================================================================
- Takes your saved plan and makes a COPY for this flight:
  1. moves the WHOLE PATH so its start is where the drone is now
     (shape, spacing and heights kept; no rotation);
  2. walls and obstacles stay where they are — the moved path is checked
     against them (0.25 m clearance);
  3. any point outside the green (or too close to an edge/obstacle) is moved
     to the nearest fine spot inside; points already fine never move;
  4. checks every point AND every leg between them.
- Each move is named on screen. If it cannot be made safe, it says why and
  the mission cannot start.
- The same function feeds ④'s map, ⑤'s map and Start — what you see is what
  flies. The copy flown is kept with the flight.
- The landing spot becomes the next flight's start.
  [missions/floor-plans.txt, missions/mission-controller.txt]

================================================================================
5. STARTING  (⑤ Fly)
================================================================================
- Session start runs the checks: radio, battery, motors unlocked, deck,
  base stations, position settled. Then you confirm the area is clear.
- Before Start, the agent lists EVERY reason it would refuse, each with what
  to do (session not ready, wrong mode, no position, drone's motors LOCKED,
  plan unsafe, battery too low for the path). Start refuses on the first.
- A LOCKED drone (its supervisor locks after some landings; only a restart
  clears it) is named, with Reset drone beside it.
  [missions/mission-controller.txt, backend/sessions-and-modes.txt]

================================================================================
6. FLYING  (the mission controller + the manual flight system)
================================================================================
- The mission controller decides WHERE NEXT and WHEN; the manual flight
  system (the tuned 50 Hz loop Manual uses) decides HOW to fly there.
- Sequence: take off to cruise height → fly to P1 at the mission's speed →
  arrive → settle → HOLD (readings and camera frames tagged with the point's
  id only while holding) → next point → … → return over the start, or land
  at the end point → land.
- Any key pressed takes over at once; the mission ends and the drone is
  yours. L lands.
- The guard watches every tick: leaving the fence → land; low battery →
  land; position lost → stop; motors not spinning (LOCKED, or all four at
  zero 1.5 s after arming) → ended as "the motors did not start".
- The app's window must keep talking to the agent; if it goes quiet, the
  drone lands.
  [missions/mission-controller.txt, backend/manual-control.txt,
   backend/flight-safety.txt]

================================================================================
7. AFTER LANDING
================================================================================
- The flight's readings are written to CSV on the laptop first, then uploaded
  (best effort, retried). The plan flown and its moves are kept beside it.
- The data pipeline turns each point's tagged readings into a verdict, shown
  under ⑤ Fly. [pipeline/data-pipeline.txt, backend/offline-sync.txt]

================================================================================
HOW IT CONNECTS, IN ONE LINE EACH
================================================================================
- Channels → sweeps decode → angles.
- Angles + geometry (② Position) → position → the green (③).
- Your plan (①) + the drone's position + the green → the plan that will
  fly (④).
- Checks + the reasons list → Start (⑤) → the mission controller flies the
  plan through the manual flight system, the guard watching.
- Landing spot → next start.
