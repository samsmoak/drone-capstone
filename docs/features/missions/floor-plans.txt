1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A ROOM is the floor plan of one real space: a closed GEOFENCE, the OBSTACLES
inside it (each with an optional height, for the room map), and (once
measured) the Lighthouse COVERAGE around it. A MISSION is a plan in a room: a
PLANNED START ("home" in the data), an ordered list of INSPECTION POINTS (x, y,
height, hold time, a stable id), an optional END POINT where the flight lands,
a cruise (takeoff) height, and whether to fly back over the start. Both are
data, checked by the agent and saved as JSON on the laptop. Nothing here flies
— see mission-controller.txt; a flight starts from wherever the drone really
is (Mission.from_start).

3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• THE FLYABLE SPACE IS COVERAGE, NEVER WALLS (2026-10-01). This drone senses
  no walls; Lighthouse tells it where it IS, wherever two base stations reach
  its deck. Room.coverage holds that outline (+ the room's height band).
  MEASURED by the survey — the drone carried round the room, every position
  with enough stations received kept (coverage.py stations_needed: the
  project's flight_guard policy, ONE by default — it said a fixed two until
  2026-10-01, which counted nothing in the lab's one-station room), their
  convex hull saved (Survey). PREDICTED from the stations' poses on the drone and Valve's
  150°x110° field of view (StationPose.sees) — shown to guide the walk, never
  flown on. One station's view is a pyramid; two overlap convexly within a
  height band, so a hull is the right shape (what it cannot show — a cabinet
  shading part of the room — the walked edge does).
• A SURVEY COUNTS ONLY POSITIONS THE DRONE STANDS BEHIND (2026-10-05):
  enough stations received AND flight_guard.position_trusted. Received alone
  was the rule until then, and a survey walked before the station was
  measured counted every drifting reading and saved an outline from -100 m
  to +100 m as the lab room's coverage — every point "moved" 1.5 m. So:
  start_survey refuses until the drone has a trusted position; the count is
  SPOTS (distinct GRID_M cells), not 10 Hz readings ("171 positions" was
  17 s); and a wrong space is dropped with Forget the measured space (POST
  /rooms/{id}/coverage/forget — the default area stands in again). The
  panel sits between the two maps on ② Check, marked optional.
• THE PLAN THAT WILL FLY is ONE function (fit.py plan_to_fly) used by BOTH the
  Check step's preview (GET …/from-drone, POST /missions/fit for an unsaved
  edit) and Session.run_mission, so what is shown is what flies. The fence is
  clipped to the coverage (Sutherland–Hodgman — the coverage is convex); every
  point outside, near an edge or an obstacle, or outside the height band is
  MOVED to the nearest fine spot (rings out to 3 m); points already fine
  NEVER move — they mark equipment. Every move is named on the page and kept
  with the flight (sessions/<id>/missions/<flight>.json "moves"). The start is
  the drone and is never moved; a leg crossing an obstacle is not re-routed —
  both stay problems in words. A point nothing fits refuses Start by name.
• THE GEOFENCE IS ALWAYS CLOSED — a rectangle, a circle, or at least 3
  corners joined last-to-first, with no crossing edges and a real area. An open
  line has no inside; a bow-tie has no single one. A circle is stored as a
  32-sided polygon, so every check handles one shape.
• THE LAYERS NEST, and each is checked against the one outside it: the ROOM'S
  MAP (the agent's default ±2 m area — validate.py outer_bound) ⊃ the
  geofence ⊃ obstacles, home, points and every LEG between them. The editor
  clamps every typed or dragged dimension to it.
• THE PLAN IS THE OPERATOR'S; THE GREEN NEVER EDITS IT (2026-10-05, Samuel:
  "the flyable space is not my architecture"). Until then the room's map WAS
  the measured coverage, so a survey saved from a drifting estimate (-100 m
  to +100 m) became a 102 m room map, put the fence 99 m out and refused the
  room. Now the measured flyable space (Room.coverage, drawn green) is read
  ONLY by the plan that will fly (validate.py flyable_bound → fit.py), which
  fits a COPY into it. The saved room and mission are never changed: opened
  again, they are exactly as drawn, and each flight keeps the fitted copy it
  flew (mission-controller.txt, "the plan flown").
• CLEARANCE, 0.25 m by default per room, applies to obstacles AND the fence's
  own edge. Derived, not guessed: half-span ~0.05 m + the manual system's drift
  notice 0.15 m + estimator noise ~0.05 m. A point on the fence line would be
  landed by the in-flight guard on ordinary drift.
• LEGS ARE CHECKED, NOT JUST POINTS: home → P1 → … → last (→ home). Two points
  can sit in clear floor with a bench between them, and in an L-shaped room a
  leg leaves the fence with both ends inside (tests/mission/test_geofence.py).
• A PLANNED START on every mission (`home`): the plan is drawn and checked
  from it while it is edited. At Start the drone's own position replaces it
  (Mission.from_start) and the mission is checked again from there — the
  0.30 m "on the home mark or refused" rule was retired 2026-09-29.
• THE END POINT (end_point_id, optional): the flight flies the points up to
  it and lands there; later points stay in the plan, are not flown, and are
  NOT held to the flying rules (validate checks flown_points only). It must
  name one of the mission's points ("end_point" error otherwise), and it wins
  over return_to_start (Mission.returns_home). Older missions read as None.
• ONE SPEED PER MISSION (speed_m_s, 2026-10-01, Samuel: "an autonomous
  flight must be extra steady"): Steady 0.10, Normal 0.15 or Brisk 0.20 m/s
  (SPEED_PRESETS_M_S); anything else is refused. Never a range: every change
  of speed is a lean, and leaning is what makes a hold unsteady. Brisk is
  manual.py MOVE_SPEED_M_S and the default, so every mission saved before
  flies as it did. estimated_duration_s travels at it (never faster than the
  flight system), so the battery refusal counts a slower mission's time; the
  controller hands it to fly_to(…, speed_m_s). The editor: "Speed between
  points".
• A POINT OR LEG MAY PASS OVER AN OBSTACLE WITH A HEIGHT (2026-10-01,
  Samuel: "above the airspace of an object if it is not too close"). Until
  then every obstacle was floor to ceiling. Now (obstacles.py clears,
  clears_leg): clear BESIDE it by the clearance, OR — when it has a height —
  at least height + clearance up. A leg is judged at its LOWER end (heights
  change linearly along it; the start and a return are at the cruise height).
  No height entered = floor to ceiling: unknown is unsafe. The START (it
  takes off from the floor) and the LANDING SPOT (the last point when not
  returning — it comes down there) must be clear BESIDE every obstacle, or
  the drone lands on it. The fit applies the same rule. Limits, said where it
  matters: under the 1.00 m ceiling only obstacles up to 0.75 m can be passed
  over; a height entered by eye 10 cm wrong is a pass 10 cm closer; the drone
  has not been tuned or tested hovering over a surface (ground effect).
• Holds are at least 5 s — story 3.4's "5 seconds of readings".
• VALIDATION RETURNS PROBLEMS, NOT AN EXCEPTION — every one, with where it is
  (a point id, "home", a leg "P1 → P2"). Errors block flying; warnings
  ("coverage not measured") are said and do not. The editor asks the agent
  (POST /missions/validate) instead of keeping a copy of the rules.
• A MISSION IS SAVED ONLY WHEN COMPLETE (2026-09-29): at least one
  inspection point, and its room saved first — not only when safe. A plan
  with a problem is saved and fixed later; validation gates flying, not
  saving. The desktop greys Save out on the same rule (auto-control.txt).
• REVISIONS: saving a change to a mission whose current revision has flown
  makes a new revision; the flight keeps a copy of the plan it flew. Saving an
  unchanged mission changes nothing.
• WRITES ARE POST, not PUT/DELETE: the desktop window's CORS allows GET and
  POST only (agent-api.txt) and that list stays narrow.
• Rooms are separate from missions, so one room's fence serves many missions;
  deleting a room that missions use is refused, naming how many.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• mission/plan NEVER imports cropwatcher.flight: a plan is drawn, checked and
  saved with no drone. tests/mission/test_boundaries.py holds it.
• x and y are absolute Lighthouse metres; heights are above the floor captured
  at takeoff (CLAUDE.md invariant 4). The editor shows feet only as labels.
• Ids go into file names: 1–64 of [A-Za-z0-9_-] for rooms and missions, 1–32
  for inspection points. An id from the API cannot walk out of the folder.
• Inspection point ids never change once saved — every reading taken at a
  point is stamped with its id (story 3.5).
• Writes are atomic (temp file, then rename); one unreadable file is skipped
  with a log line and never hides the rest of a list.
