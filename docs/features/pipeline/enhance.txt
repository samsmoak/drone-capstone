1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Story 4.3: clearer images, so small defects show. The AI deck records 324×244
8-bit grayscale; the lab's frames are DARK (median brightness 61 of 255).
Every frame gets an enhanced copy beside it, and its quality — sharpness,
brightness, black and white clipping — measured on the ORIGINAL.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• CONTRAST, NOT RESOLUTION. CLAHE moves pixel values tile by tile and draws
  none; a super-resolution network enlarges noise on frames this dark and can
  invent texture — an invented crack is a false alarm.
• clip 2.0 on 4×4 tiles: the most contrast (×1.21) for the grain of OpenCV's
  default 8×8 (grain on flat areas ×2.25 either way) — RESULTS.txt.
• QUALITY LIMITS from looking at the worst frames: under sharpness 25 or mean
  15 is a hand over the lens or a wall out of focus. 2.2 % of real frames.
  An unusable frame is still enhanced and kept, with its reason; no finding
  reads it as evidence.
• NO COLOUR: the frames hold none; colourising would guess it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• The original is byte-identical after the stage (tested); copies go only
  under the workdir.
• One EnhancedFrame per frame, in order; an unreadable frame is path None with
  a note, the rest carry on.
• An enhanced image is labelled as one everywhere it is shown.
• 20 frames in 37 ms on the development Mac.
