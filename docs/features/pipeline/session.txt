--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The owner, 2026-10-09: "data processing should happen in both Auto and Manual
— as long as a session is started we process whatever data comes, not only
data in flight." A session records its own samples once a second from Start
session to End session (samples.csv, the same file the web's session vitals
come from) and the camera throughout. A flight's result covers its flight; the
session's run covers everything around them: its samples cleaned, the frames
taken outside its flights enhanced, and the stretches when the drone sat on the
ground judged for anomalies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• THE SAME FOUR STAGES, one swapped. The cleaner, enhancer and interpreter are
  the flight's; the classifier is ground@1. A flight is judged against the
  board cooling in the propellers' air at 10 Hz; on the ground the board does
  the opposite (it warms, towards a level) at 1 Hz. Same model family —
  T∞ + A·e^(−t/τ) fits warming as well as cooling — fitted per ground stretch.
• ONLY THE GROUND IS JUDGED. Every reading in a flight, or within
  GROUND_SETTLE_S (10 s) of one, is left out: the flights have results of their
  own at ten times the rate, and a flight's cooling is not a ground event.
• QUEUED AT END SESSION, read from the DPP switch as it ends, after the samples
  file is closed — in Auto and Manual alike. A session the app closed before
  its job finished is processed again when the app starts.
• ITS OWN TABLE, pipeline_session_results, and findings with scope 'session'
  and no flight. A session finding is deleted with its session (a trigger: the
  findings' session link is "set null", which would orphan it).
• THE WORDS SAY SO: "Warmer than expected on the ground", "its own trend
  there", never a cooling curve, an inspection point or a height — on the
  ground z is the floor's Lighthouse height, not a height above it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• A SESSION'S READINGS COME ONCE A SECOND. Every limit counted in seconds
  scales with FlightContext.period_s: the cleaner's gap (2.5 periods), its
  plausibility horizon, and the seconds its sentences quote ("14 readings
  (14.0 s"). A 10 Hz constant used at 1 Hz flagged every reading a gap, and
  said 1.4 s for 14 s (found 2026-10-09, in the browser).
• Ground thresholds are measured, not copied from flights: a normal ground
  block wanders up to 0.93 °C and 0.16 hPa, so the minimums are 1.0 °C and
  0.2 hPa. At the flight's 0.8 °C one normal session raised an event.
  0 false events on 179 sessions; every 2–5 °C bump and 0.3–0.5 hPa step
  planted into 31 of them found (MEASUREMENTS.txt, "Grid").
• A reading's index is its samples.csv row, 1-based — the same `seq` as
  public.session_samples, so the web tints exactly the rows a finding names.
• A flight that never closed (the app quit mid-flight) does not mark the rest
  of the session as flying: the motor thrust decides there.
