1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Story 4.3: "As an inspector, I want clearer images, so that even small defects
are visible." For every frame of an inspection point the stage writes an
enhanced copy, twice the size, beside the original and never over it:

  ESPCN ×2       a small super-resolution network: 324×244 → 648×488
  CLAHE 1.5      local contrast, 8×8 tiles — most of the visible gain
  edge sharpen   unsharp 0.6, only where the gradient is a real edge

Classify can then use either image. 20 frames take 0.83 s; the model is
0.09 MB. ml/upscaling-eval/before-after-camera-frames.jpg is the PDF's
before/after sheet.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• CONTRAST, NOT RESOLUTION, IS WHAT SHOWS MORE. On the camera's frames ESPCN
  and FSRCNN tie bicubic (ΔPSNR −0.03 / +0.02 dB): the frames hold nothing
  finer than the camera's resolution for a network to recover. ESPCN stays
  for its ×2 size and costs 36 ms a frame. Chosen by the team from the
  sheets; ml/upscaling-eval/RESULTS.txt, WHY THIS ONE.
• SHARPEN EDGES ONLY. A plain unsharp mask turns sensor noise into grain;
  weighting it by edge strength (none below 3 grey levels/px, full above 8 —
  measured on the camera frames) cut the grain score from 3.70 to 2.37 at
  the same edge sharpness.
• NO DENOISER. Non-local means scored the same and looked cleaner, but it
  softens faint real texture — a hairline crack is that — and took 1.8 s for
  20 frames, at the edge of the 2 s budget.
• ×2, NOT ×4, AND NO GAN. ×4 adds pixels and no detail; Real-ESRGAN invented
  panes, dividers and stripes that are not in the scene.
• RUN AS MEASURED, WITH OPENCV — AN EXCEPTION TO THE CONTRACT'S "ONNX". The
  model is TensorFlow's .pb run by OpenCV dnn_superres, the runtime the
  ticket named for ESPCN; opencv-contrib is already on the contract's
  dependency list (rule 11). Converting to ONNX would ship a model nobody
  scored. MODELS.json records the runtime per model.
• ONE COPY OF THE STEPS. ml/upscaling-eval/methods.py imports espcn.py's
  functions rather than keeping its own; on all 15 frames the evaluation's
  winner is byte-identical to the stage's output.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• THE ENHANCED IMAGE IS NEVER EVIDENCE ON ITS OWN, AND IS LABELLED "ENHANCED"
  WHEREVER IT IS SHOWN. It keeps faint grain on smooth surfaces (2.37 against
  bicubic's 1.18) and no step recovers detail the camera did not capture.
  No screen shows one yet; the first that does carries the label.
• NEVER READ BRIGHTNESS FROM AN ENHANCED FRAME. CLAHE equalises contrast tile
  by tile: on the fixture's smooth ramp it flattened the gradient into blotchy
  grey and put a halo along strong edges. Intensity, and any comparison of
  two regions, comes from the original.
• Classify must be trained on the images it is given in use. Whichever it
  takes (ImageVerdict.source), it trains on the same kind — enhanced means
  espcn-clahe@1's output, not another enhancer's.
• Bump `version` whenever the output could change: a step, a constant, the
  model, or OpenCV's major version (pinned >=5.0,<6 in pyproject.toml — the
  method was measured on 5.0).
• A missing or altered model raises StageError and the runner uses the
  originals. The frozen agent's selftest loads the model, so a bundle without
  it fails the build instead of every flight quietly going unenhanced.
• One bad frame gets path=None and a note; the others are still enhanced.
