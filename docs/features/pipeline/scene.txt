--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The camera is 324 × 244 grayscale: a warm pump looks like a cold one. What it
CAN see is whether its view changed while a reading departed — a person in
front of the drone, a door, a light. scene@1 measures that per frame, and a
finding whose stretch contains a changed frame says so ("supports"). A steady
view "cannot tell"; nothing ever "contradicts" a reading (the owner: the
telemetry is the source of truth, an image only supports it).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• COMPARED WITH THE FRAME BEFORE, from the same place (within 5 cm, 1.5 s),
  shrunk, blurred and standardised — an exposure change alone is not a change.
• ALIGNED FIRST (phase correlation): the lab's 0.35–0.45 "steady" pairs were
  the drone turning a few degrees in place; a shifted view is not something
  new in it. A shift past a quarter of the frame is a different view: no score.
• SCENE_CHANGE = 0.45: a person-sized occluder 93 %, a lit patch 89 %, an
  exposure change 1.8 %, 1.4 % of steady pairs (mostly the drone handled).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• A frame with no comparable predecessor has NO score and says why (moved,
  too long after, unreadable) — never a guessed 0.
• Only a changed frame INSIDE a finding's stretch supports it.
• The words always say to look at the frames: the score is a pointer, not a
  verdict.
