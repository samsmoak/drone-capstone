--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Story 4.9. Until now a mission's verdicts came after landing. Now, the moment
the drone finishes holding at an inspection point, the flight SO FAR — still
being recorded — goes through every stage once, and that point's verdict
(normal, anomaly, not enough data) appears beside it in the Control page's
Progress list, with any finding that touches it, while the drone flies on.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• THE WHOLE FLIGHT SO FAR, not the point alone: a stretch can start in
  transit, and the expected cooling curve needs everything flown.
• IN A CHILD PROCESS at a lower priority, like every pipeline run — never a
  thread beside the 50 Hz flight loop, which shares one interpreter lock.
• LIVE GOES FIRST: a live job is queued ahead of anything waiting (another
  flight's or a session's processing) — it is wanted now.
• NEVER UPLOADED. The result after landing (run_flight) replaces it; a live
  verdict is a preview, said in the app as "judging…", then its verdict.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• A flight being recorded may end in a half-written row: the live load skips
  a row csv.DictReader filled with None, instead of failing.
• Only a flight the DPP switch marked for processing is judged in flight.
• A live job's verdict for a flight that has since ended is not shown.
