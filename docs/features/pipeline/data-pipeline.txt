--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Story 4.5: "A single command turns a recorded flight into a result with a
verdict for each inspection point." `cropwatcher process --flight <id>` loads
the flight's readings, its session's frames and the plan it flew, runs
clean → enhance → classify → interpret ONCE over the whole flight, and writes
<data folder>/results/<flight id>/result.json and the enhanced frames. The
owner's ask (2026-10-09): the dirty data against the clean, the grayscale
frame against the enhanced, and what it means — by BLOCKS of change, not every
wiggle, with the readings as the evidence and the frames as support.

3. DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• ON THE LAPTOP, IN THE AGENT — not Supabase. Edge Functions are Deno only with
  2 s of CPU a request; the laptop has every frame the moment the drone lands
  (the cloud gets 2 a second); only the laptop can steer the drone, which
  stories 4.9 and 4.10 need. PORTS AND ADAPTERS (FlightSource, ResultSink),
  stages chosen in one composition root: a cloud worker later is two adapters.
• THE UNIT OF WORK IS THE FLIGHT (contract v2, 2026-10-09 — CHANGED from one
  inspection point). A block of unusual readings can start in transit and end
  at a point; a stage that sees one point at a time cannot find it, and v1
  never analysed transit at all. Readings and frames carry their point_id;
  interpret gives every point its verdict from them.
• FRAMES ARE PER SESSION, so a flight's frames are the session's frames taken
  between its first and last reading.
• A STAGE THAT FAILS OR BREAKS THE CONTRACT never takes the flight with it:
  clean failing leaves every point insufficient_data (unchecked readings are
  not analysed); enhance failing falls back to originals; classify failing
  labels everything unknown; interpret failing leaves every point
  insufficient_data; each failure recorded with its reason.
• NO INVENTED THRESHOLD. Every number in a stage cites a measurement file:
  ml/sensor-faults/MEASUREMENTS.txt, ml/enhance-eval/RESULTS.txt,
  ml/anomaly-eval/MEASUREMENTS.txt. Severities are tied to the measured wander
  of normal flights and stay PROVISIONAL until a real heat source is measured.
• THE TELEMETRY IS THE SOURCE OF TRUTH; AN IMAGE ONLY SUPPORTS IT (the owner).
  No finding is made from a frame; frames are attached as evidence.
• RUNTIME DEPENDENCIES: numpy (clean, classify), opencv-python-headless
  (enhance). OpenCV doubled the frozen agent (43 → 86 MB), verified by
  build_sidecar.py's self-test. scipy and ruptures are NOT shipped (ruptures
  is a test dependency, the reference PELT).
• THE DPP SWITCH (2026-09-29; moved and defaulted 2026-10-09): at the TOP of
  the Control page in Auto and Manual alike, ON BY DEFAULT IN BOTH (Manual was
  off until the owner asked). Settable before a session starts or during one;
  the choice lasts until the session ends; audited; read as a flight BEGINS.
• `cropwatcher process --all` processes every recorded flight with no result
  or one from an older pipeline, and queues each for the web; a flight with no
  readings is skipped. Run again, it does only what is left.
• A CHILD PROCESS, one flight at a time, lower priority, 15 minutes at most —
  never in the agent's process (the interpreter lock beside the 50 Hz loop; a
  hung stage must never take the radio with it).
• PROCESSING RESUMES WHEN THE APP STARTS AGAIN (2026-10-09, the owner's ask):
  flights left pending, queued or running are queued again, in the
  background; a FAILED one is not retried unasked. Each laptop processes its
  OWN flights — it holds every reading and frame. CROPWATCHER_RESUME=0 turns
  it off; the tests turn it off (tests/conftest.py).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• The pipeline imports nothing from flight, session, api, sync or camera —
  tests/pipeline/test_conformance.py rule 1, per implementation. (safety is
  allowed: the cleaner imports the flight guard's speed limit, one number.)
• Every implementation in tests/pipeline/conformance.py passes the contract's
  checked rules on the whole flight, on each point, and on an empty point.
• Temperatures arrive in the flight's own unit; an absolute value takes
  × 9/5 + 32, a difference × 9/5 only — °F and °C give the same flags, the
  same events, the same severities (tested in each stage).
• Re-running a flight replaces its result.json (atomic) and, once uploaded,
  its row and findings on the web; a finding keeps its id.
• The resume never runs on import of api/rest.py (built at import): a test
  must not start processing the laptop's real flights.
