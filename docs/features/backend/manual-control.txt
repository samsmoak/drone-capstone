MANUAL CONTROL — assisted flying from held keys
===============================================

WHAT IT IS

Manual mode does not send raw thrust. The window reports WHICH KEYS ARE HELD
about ten times a second; the agent runs a 50 Hz loop that turns that intent
into a height-holding setpoint. Let go of everything and the drone holds where
it is — it does not sink, and with base stations it does not wander off either.

Pressing Start does not take off. It idles the propellers on the ground at
IDLE_THRUST (11000, just above cflib's "10001 = next to no power") so the
operator can see the drone is live. It leaves the ground only while W is held.


WHERE IT LIVES

  backend/agent/cropwatcher/flight/manual.py  the loop and the control state
  backend/agent/cropwatcher/flight/link.py    reads the position the spot comes from
  backend/agent/cropwatcher/flight/geometry.py  re-measures where the stations are
  backend/agent/cropwatcher/api/rest.py     /ws/live carries the intent
  desktop/src/App.tsx                       key state -> intent
  backend/agent/tests/test_manual.py


UNASSISTED — BAROMETER HEIGHT HOLD (no base stations)

The agent switches the drone to the complementary estimator
(stabilizer.estimator = 1), whose height comes from the BAROMETER, waits 1.5 s,
and reads the floor from it. Flight then uses send_zdistance_setpoint(roll,
pitch, yawrate, z): W/S move a height target at 0.15 m/s up to 0.80 m, letting go
holds it, arrows tilt up to 5 degrees, A/D yaw. Land lowers the target at
0.25 m/s, settles 0.6 s at the floor, then stops — a descent, never a drop. The
Kalman estimator is restored when the flight ends.

EXPERIMENTAL. A barometer height indoors wanders by tens of centimetres and prop
wash disturbs it; the drone also has no position hold, so it drifts. Base
stations remain the real fix. The pitch sign differs between the generic and
legacy setpoint packets — PITCH_SIGN in manual.py must be checked in the lab.

Replaced (2026-09-16): a raw-throttle law. Its slow ramp through liftoff let the
drone skid on a leg and tumble, and letting go of W held nothing.


HOLDING THE SPOT (assisted only)

Letting go of the arrows used to send a velocity of ZERO, which asks the drone
to stop — not to stay. A drone told to stop drifts: every error in the estimate
and every draught moves it, and nothing brings it back. That is the sideways
wander manual flight had.

Now, once the arrows are released AND the glide has decayed below 2 cm/s, the
loop reads the position the drone reports, keeps it, and commands it absolutely
with send_position_setpoint(x, y, z, yaw) until the operator asks for something
else. The firmware's own position controller flies back to it at 100 Hz. W and S
still move the height while the spot is held, so climbing straight up holds x
and y instead of sliding.

The spot is taken ONCE and never follows the drone. An anchor that tracked the
live position would follow the drift, which corrects nothing.

The agent does not change how hard the correction pushes, and there is no
"react harder past N centimetres" gain here. An absolute position IS the
strongest correction this link can send, and the firmware already pushes
proportionally harder the further off it is, at 100 Hz rather than the radio's
10. What the distance is used for is KNOWING: drift_m is measured every tick,
recorded in the trace beside the spot being held, and logged past
DRIFT_NOTICE_M (0.15 m) — far below the +/-2.00 m manual geofence, which is
too coarse to notice a hover sliding across a room.

Unassisted flight never holds a spot: without base stations x and y are dead
reckoning, and anchoring to them would fly the drone into the drift. With no
position reported at all the loop falls back to the old zero-velocity hover
rather than inventing a coordinate.


THE GLIDE

Nothing the keys command changes in a step. A press used to set the climb speed
instantly and a release stopped it instantly — a jolt at both ends, and the
height controller answered with the motor hammering in the 2026-09-17 traces.
The climb speed, the assisted arrow velocities, the unassisted tilt and the yaw
rate all ease toward what the keys ask at a bounded rate (CLIMB_ACCEL_M_S2 0.30,
MOVE_ACCEL_M_S2 0.40, TILT_RATE_DEG_S 12, YAW_ACCEL_DEG_S2 90). Releasing W
eases to a stop within about 4 cm and then holds exactly. Land eases out of a
climb before easing into the descent, so pressing Land mid-climb is smooth.

See flight-tuning.txt for the firmware-side half of the same fix.


THE NUMBERS

  CLIMB_ACCEL_M_S2    0.30          easing, both modes
  TICK_HZ             50            setpoints per second
  HEARTBEAT_TIMEOUT_S 0.5           no word from the window -> land
  IDLE_THRUST         11000         props turning, no lift
  CLIMB_RATE_M_S      0.15          W / S, gentle by design
  LIFTOFF_HEIGHT_M    0.05          above this counts as flying
  MAX_HEIGHT_M        1.00          ceiling (assisted)
  MAX_UNASSISTED_HEIGHT_M 0.80      ceiling on the barometer
  LAND_RATE_M_S       0.25          barometer descent
  MOVE_SPEED_M_S      0.20          arrows, body frame
  STOPPED_M_S         0.02          below this the glide is over, take the spot
  DRIFT_NOTICE_M      0.15          off the spot and still correcting -> logged
  MAX_PLAUSIBLE_SPEED_M_S 2.0       above this a fix is the estimator, not the
                                    drone (shared with safety/flight_guard.py)
  YAW_RATE_DEG_S      30            A / D
  LAND_S              3.0           graceful descent

  states: IDLE -> ARMED -> FLYING -> LANDING -> LANDED / STOPPED

KEYS (all shown on screen, always)

  Up / Down arrow   forward / back
  Left / Right      left / right
  W / S             rise / descend, gently
  A / D             rotate left / right
  L                 land gracefully
  Emergency stop    hold one second, no key binding


DECISIONS

- Held keys change a height TARGET at 0.15 m/s; they do not change thrust. Raw
  thrust has no height hold: releasing the key drops the drone, and holding it
  climbs without limit. The first version did exactly that.
- Releasing every key holds the current height. The earlier code decayed the
  thrust, which read as "the drone is sinking for no reason".
- "Cut motors" was removed as an operator control and replaced with Land.
  Cutting the motors in the air drops the drone; it survives only as the
  hold-to-activate Emergency stop.
- The window never generates a setpoint stream. It can be closed, refreshed or
  frozen; the drone's behaviour must not depend on its frame rate.


INVARIANTS

- Send ONE zero-thrust setpoint before any other. The firmware's rpyt commander
  locks thrust until it sees thrust = 0, then silently holds every later
  setpoint at zero. Missed, it cost a lab session: the app said the props were
  turning and no key moved the drone. arm() sends it; a test pins it for both
  control laws. The lab's hop_test.py and keyboard_fly.py do the same.

- Schedule ticks against a DEADLINE, not a fixed sleep. sleep(1/50) after the
  work measured 38 Hz end to end, not 50; deadline scheduling measured 50.
- The heartbeat is the dead-man switch. Half a second of silence from the window
  lands the drone — that covers a crashed window, a locked laptop and a closed
  lid, all of which leave a drone in the air otherwise.
- Space is blocked on keydown AND keyup. WebKit activates a focused button on
  Space keyup, so blocking keydown alone still leaves a focused dangerous button
  one Space away.
- A window that loses focus never sees keyup, so blur releases every key. The
  drone then holds its height instead of drifting on with a key stuck down.
- The same guards run in manual as in auto (flight-safety.txt). Manual is not a
  bypass; it is a different source of intent.

- NEVER act on a position that moved faster than the drone can fly. A fix that
  jumps further between two ticks than MAX_PLAUSIBLE_SPEED_M_S (2.0 m/s) allows
  is the ESTIMATOR moving, not the drone, and flying to it drives the drone hard
  towards a place it already is. Measured 2026-09-21 with stale base-station
  geometry: the estimate moved 21 cm in one 0.1 s step (2.1 m/s) with the drone
  sitting still on the floor and both stations received 96 % of the time. The
  arrows command 0.20 m/s, so the bound is ten times any real manoeuvre.
  A rejected fix keeps the previous spot; a reading that PERSISTS is believed
  after about 0.1 s, so a drone genuinely carried somewhere is not ignored.

- Hold the spot only on geometry that has been measured in THIS room. The
  anchor is exactly as good as the position estimate under it. Stale geometry
  does not fail loudly — the stations simply disagree — and the anchor would
  then hold a coordinate that is quietly wrong. Run `cropwatcher geometry`
  after any station is moved, nudged or re-aimed.
