GALLERY — albums of photos and videos
=====================================

WHAT IT IS

/gallery shows albums as cards — a mosaic of each album's first photos, its
category, and how many photos and videos are inside — with a category filter.
/gallery/<slug> opens an album: its videos first, then its photos in a masonry
layout at their real proportions, each opening full screen. Operators manage it
at /admin/gallery.

DECISIONS

- Albums, not one long wall of photos: grouped like projects, so a visitor picks
  a subject first. Category is free text, so a new kind of album needs no
  migration.
- Videos are YouTube links, played through youtube-nocookie and only loaded on
  click — the player is heavy and starts tracking; most visitors never press
  play. Uploading video files was not built: storage and transcoding cost more
  than a link.
- Image width and height are stored with each photo (read from the file header
  by the seed, in the browser by the admin), so the masonry lays out before the
  images load and nothing jumps.
- The seed downloads its own images, so it can be re-run on any machine. It
  replaces the items of the albums it owns and leaves every other album alone.


INVARIANTS

- next/image loads from this project's storage host and from i.ytimg.com (video
  posters) only — next.config.ts remotePatterns.
- Anyone reads published albums and their items; drafts and every write are
  operator-only (RLS). Deleting an album deletes its items (on delete cascade).
  Verified 2026-09-17: anon cannot see a draft; operator create/add/delete work;
  items cascade.
- Only public material goes in the gallery bucket: a public URL cannot be
  revoked.
- Verified: /gallery, /gallery/<slug> render with no console errors in both
  themes and no horizontal overflow at 390 and 768 px.
