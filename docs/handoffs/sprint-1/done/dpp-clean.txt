WHAT HAPPENED, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Kevin's hampel@1 met this ticket's acceptance on the one flight in the repo.
Scored on 66 real flights from the lab laptop, it caught 97.4 % of planted
faults, raised 0.14 % false flags and 632 flags inside the planted hand-warmer
ramps (where the rule is zero). Measuring why showed one cause for most of it:
corrected_temp steps every time the correction engine's thermal_state
switches, and a short held step is what a median calls a spike. robust@1
keeps everything hampel@1 got right — the five checks, the window, K, the
floors, no rate-of-change rule, flag-never-edit — and judges corrected_temp by
the engine's offset within each thermal state instead, counts stuck runs
across gaps, and adds position and battery. On the same flights and seeds it
catches 100 %, raises 0.011 % false flags (all of them the propellers' own
pressure pulses, flagged on purpose) and 0 ramp flags, in half the time.
Every number: ml/sensor-faults/MEASUREMENTS.txt.
