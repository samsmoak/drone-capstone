THE WHOLE SYSTEM, IN ONE PAGE — read this first
───────────────────────────────────────────────

DroneDeck flies a Crazyflie around a room, stops at pieces of equipment, and
records temperature, pressure and camera frames at each. It has three parts:

  1  PLANNING    the operator draws a room (geofence, obstacles) and a mission
                 in it: an ordered list of INSPECTION POINTS.     Samuel, built
  2  FLYING      the mission controller flies the mission through the manual
                 flight system. While the drone HOLDS at a point, every
                 reading and camera frame is stamped with that point's id.
                                                                  Hannah
  3  PROCESSING  the data pipeline: each point's stamped readings and frames
                 go through four stages, and the point gets a verdict.
                                                      Samuel, Kevin, Reagan

THE JOB, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

The AI deck's camera gives 324×244 GRAYSCALE frames. For every frame of an
inspection point, write an enhanced copy (upscaled, clearer) under
ctx.workdir, and return where it is. THE ORIGINAL IS NEVER TOUCHED. The
classifier and the inspector can then use either.


TWO FACTS THAT SHAPE THIS TICKET

1. THE CAMERA HAS NOT DELIVERED A WHOLE REAL FRAME YET
   (docs/features/desktop/camera.txt). Until it does, work with:
   - the agent's test pattern (cropwatcher/camera/source.py), for the
     plumbing;
   - THE TRAINING PHOTOS FROM STORY 4.1 (Hannah and Yordanos), shrunk to
     324×244 grayscale. For judging quality, these are better than real
     frames: you have the full-resolution original to compare against.

2. SUPER-RESOLUTION INVENTS DETAIL. A GAN-based upscaler draws plausible
   texture that was never in the scene. On an inspection drone, an invented
   crack is a false alarm, and a smoothed-over one is a miss. So:
   - The enhanced image is LABELLED AS ENHANCED everywhere it is shown.
   - It is never the only evidence for a verdict. The classify owner decides
     which image the model uses, and must train on the same kind.
   - Your evaluation must look for invented detail, not just sharpness.

CANDIDATE METHODS (all free, all run locally, none needs Docker)

Evaluate at least the first three, then pick one by the numbers.

  CLAHE + unsharp mask
      Classical local-contrast boost, no upscaling.
      Runtime: opencv-contrib-python-headless.
      THE BASELINE TO BEAT. Instant, invents nothing, and often shows more on
      low-contrast grayscale than a ×4 upscale does.

  FSRCNN / ESPCN ×2, ×4
      Small super-resolution networks.
      Runtime: OpenCV dnn_superres (THE CONTRIB BUILD, not plain
      opencv-python).
      Tiny model files, fast on CPU. Pretrained .pb files are linked from
      OpenCV's dnn_superres docs; record the source and license in
      MODELS.json.

  Real-ESRGAN, realesr-general-x4v3
      Small GAN upscaler, BSD-3-Clause.
      Runtime: export to ONNX, then onnxruntime.
      The sharpest-looking result, and the one most likely to invent detail.
      Check it hardest.

  LapSRN, EDSR
      Larger super-resolution networks. Runtime: OpenCV dnn_superres.
      EDSR is slow on CPU. Only try it if the small ones fall short.

  Colorize (stretch)
      Zhang et al. colorization (OpenCV sample model), or DeOldify (MIT).
      Runtime: OpenCV dnn, or ONNX.
      COLORS ARE GUESSED, NOT MEASURED. Grayscale holds no color, so
      "accurately" in the PDF's criterion cannot be met in general. Make it a
      separate enhancer, never the default, and label its output "colorized
      (estimated)".

Keep evaluation code (PyTorch and the like) in ml/upscaling-eval/. Only the
runtime path goes into stages/enhance/, within the dependency list in the
contract (rule 11).
