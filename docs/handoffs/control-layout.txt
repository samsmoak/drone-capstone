WHAT IT IS
──────────

Nothing on the Control page is squeezed, overlapping or clipped at any window
width, in either theme — and that is now a STANDING rule every screen is
measured against (../design/layout.txt).


SCOPE — DELIVERED
─────────────────

  [x] Tab strips WRAP: the actions (10 Hz, Full screen) drop under the tabs
      instead of crushing them; tabs never shrink and scroll sideways if
      even they do not fit. (At a 1024 px window the console's tab list was
      97 px wide holding 177 px of tabs.)
  [x] The attitude readouts wrap under the instrument instead of overlapping
      it; the battery & motor test splits in two by the PANEL's width, not
      the window's.
  [x] Auto shows the flow and the monitor side by side from 1280 px, stacked
      below; its divider stops at 45–70 %.
  [x] SplitPane clamps to bounds that move with the container.
  [x] The standing rule: desktop measured at 720 / 900 / 1024 / 1280 /
      1600 px and the website at 390 / 768 / 1024 / 1440, both themes,
      layout by container width (../design/layout.txt, PREFERENCES.txt).
  [x] The harness: ?only=, ?theme=, ?map=2d|3d, and sections for ③ Fly
      landed and full screen.
