--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE SHARED LAYER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
web/components/ui/raw-readings.tsx   RawReadings — every stored reading of a
    flight (FLIGHT_COLUMNS) or a session (SESSION_COLUMNS, indexKey "seq"),
    25 fixed rows a page, marks tinted. Not for a summary: it is the evidence.
web/components/processing/error-table.tsx   ErrorTable — only what went wrong,
    one row per mark, in time order; "Show" opens a RawReadings by its id.
web/components/ui/mark-legend.tsx   MarkLegend, MarkShape — the key to the
    marks, and the severity's shape in its group's colour.
web/components/ui/hint.tsx   useHint — a tooltip that works on hover, focus and
    tap, fixed so a scrolling box cannot clip it.
web/lib/pipeline.ts   anomalyMarks, faultMarks, marksOf, groupTint — the one
    place a mark's colour, depth and words are decided.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Tokens only — no raw colours or ad-hoc sizes.
• Every theme the project supports, every component, every time.
• COLOUR IS THE DATA, DEPTH IS HOW SEVERE (the owner, 2026-10-09). Anything
  the pipeline highlights takes its data group's token (--group-temperature,
  --group-pressure, --group-fault, --group-position — app/globals.css) at
  the severity's depth (lib/pipeline SHADE: 14 / 28 / 48 %), with the
  severity's shape and word beside it, and the same legend. Status colours
  (--status-*) stay for health and states, never for a data group.
• A box that scrolls sideways is `relative`: an absolutely positioned child
  (screen-reader text) otherwise escapes it and widens the page (found
  2026-10-09: 139 px on the error table's Show buttons).
