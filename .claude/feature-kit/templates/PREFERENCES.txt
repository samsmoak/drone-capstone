design approach, an architectural choice, a way of laying something out — log
it here with what was asked and where it was applied. On the THIRD, ask once
whether to make it standing. If yes, move it into the design or platform doc it
belongs to (so every screen obeys it without being asked) and mark it STANDING
here with a pointer.
