ENHANCE EVAL — the settings the pipeline's stage 2 runs, and why
==============================================================

The numbers behind backend/agent/cropwatcher/pipeline/stages/enhance/clahe.py
(clahe@1). Measured 2026-10-09 on the development Mac, on 600 frames sampled
(seed 0) from the 8 748 the AI deck recorded on the lab laptop — real frames,
324×244 8-bit grayscale. The frames are not committed: the repository is
public and they show the lab.

    cd backend/agent && .venv/bin/python ../../ml/enhance-eval/evaluate.py \
        --data "<data folder>" --n 600 --out <a folder outside the repo>


WHAT THE FRAMES NEED

  Brightness (mean pixel, 0..255)   p5 31 · p25 55 · p50 61 · p75 63 · p95 100
  Sharpness (var. of Laplacian)     p1 21 · p5 56 · p25 110 · p50 182 · p95 244
  Crushed black / blown white       p95 3.4 % / 4.4 % of pixels

  The frames are DARK (median 61 of 255) and low in contrast, with ceiling
  lights blown out. Resolution is not what hides detail; contrast is. So the
  enhancer is CLAHE — it moves pixel values within each tile and never draws
  one that was not there. A super-resolution network (FSRCNN, ESPCN,
  Real-ESRGAN) enlarges noise on frames this dark and can invent texture;
  Kevin's comparison of those (origin/feat/dpp-enhance, ml/upscaling-eval)
  can still replace this behind the same contract if it shows real detail.
  No colour: the frames hold none.


SETTINGS  (median over the 600 frames)

  setting          contrast ×   entropy, bits   grain on flat areas ×   ms/frame
  clip 1.5, 8×8    1.08         +0.35           1.98                    0.13
  clip 2.0, 8×8    1.12         +0.43           2.26                    0.13
  clip 3.0, 8×8    1.19         +0.62           2.96                    0.14
  clip 2.0, 4×4    1.21         +0.67           2.25                    0.11   ← chosen
  clip 3.0, 4×4    1.31         +0.87           2.87                    0.11

  "Grain on flat areas" is the Laplacian's spread where the original is
  smooth (the flattest 30 %): what an enhancer adds there is noise, not
  detail. clip 2.0 on 4×4 tiles gives the most contrast for the grain of the
  8×8 default; clip 3.0 buys a little more contrast for a third more grain.
  Seen by eye on an 8-frame before/after sheet: shadows under the tables, the
  far wall and a person in frame open up; the floor's texture grows grainier.

  → CLIP_LIMIT = 2.0, TILE_GRID = (4, 4).


QUALITY LIMITS  (measured on the ORIGINAL)

  The 12 least sharp and the 12 darkest of the 600, looked at one by one:
  every frame under sharpness 25 or mean 15 is the camera covered by a hand,
  or out of focus against a wall — nothing to read. Two at sharpness ~80 and
  mean 12–13 are a hand in front of the lens with a strip of ceiling light.

  → MIN_SHARPNESS = 25, MIN_BRIGHTNESS = 15, more than 50 % of pixels crushed
    to black (≤ 5) or blown to white (≥ 250) is unreadable.
  → 13 of the 600 frames (2.2 %) are marked unusable. They are still
    enhanced and kept; the reason goes with them, and no finding reads one
    as evidence.


TIME

  20 frames, enhanced and written, 37 ms on the development Mac (the
  ticket's target: under 2 s on the lab laptop — re-measure there).
