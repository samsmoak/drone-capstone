  EspcnEnhancer, espcn-clahe@1 (#103); the agent ships clahe@1 instead

THE SET

  camera-frames: 15 frames from the AI deck's camera (screenshots of
  2026-10-06; manifests/camera-frames.csv), protocol "self": the truth is the

WHY THIS ONE

  1. It shows more, where the networks alone do not. ESPCN ×2 and FSRCNN ×2
     tie bicubic (ΔPSNR −0.03 and +0.02 dB) and look the same as it on the
     sheets: these frames hold no detail finer than the camera's for a network
     to recover. The visible gain comes from contrast: CLAHE, then sharpening.
  2. It keeps the sharpness of the first favourite with a third less grain.
     espcn-x2+clahe-2.0-0.6 keeps edges at 1.07 with flat texture 3.70 —
     speckle across every smooth surface. This keeps 1.04 at 2.37: CLAHE 1.5
     lifts less noise, and the edge-only sharpen leaves smooth areas alone.
  3. It smooths nothing away. The denoised candidates tie it on grain
     (nlm-3 …+edge-0.6, 2.37) and look cleaner still, but non-local means
     softens faint real texture — the mesh fence in frame 1 — and on an
     inspection drone a hairline crack is exactly that kind of texture. They
     also take 1.8 s for 20 frames, at the edge of the 2 s budget.
  4. Fast and small. 36 ms a frame here; the stage itself — read, enhance,
     write the PNG — takes 0.83 s for 20 real frames. The model is 0.09 MB.
  5. The team chose it from the sheets.

  REJECTED
  realesrgan-x4   Invents structure: windows' panes became blank panels with
                  dividers the scene does not have, mesh was wiped flat,
                  hazard stripes redrawn. 14 s for 20 frames; 4.9 MB.
  ×4 of any kind  No detail for it to show: 16× the pixels of the frame and
                  4× the file of ×2, and ESPCN ×4 looks like bicubic ×4.
  clahe 2.0 rows  The most grain of the CLAHE family (2.71 to 5.03).


KNOWN LIMITS

  - CLAHE DOES NOT KEEP BRIGHTNESS. It equalises contrast tile by tile: on
    the pipeline fixture's smooth ramp it flattens the gradient into blotchy
    near-even grey and puts a light halo along strong edges. Never measure
    brightness or compare two regions' intensity on an enhanced frame; use
    the original.
  - Some grain remains on smooth surfaces: flat texture 2.37 against
    bicubic's 1.18. It is fainter than the first favourite's, not gone.
  - Faint edges are sharpened less: the edge weight ramps in from 3 grey
    levels per pixel, a threshold measured on CLAHE 2.0 output. CLAHE 1.5
    makes edges a little less steep, so it sharpens slightly more gently
    than at 2.0 — on the safe side.
  - The truth is soft. The camera-frames truth has been through a viewer's
    enlargement and our shrink, which favours methods that add nothing.
  - The networks were trained on photos shrunk by bicubic resizing; prepare.py
    shrinks by area averaging, closer to a sensor. The mismatch costs them a
    little in the table.
  - 15 frames, not the ticket's 30, and no sharper reference. Re-run on the
    story 4.1 photos (README.txt, RUN) when they arrive: protocol
    "reference" can show whether a network earns more there.
  - Timed on this i9, not the lab laptop. Re-run score.py and the stage
    timing there; the 2 s budget has room for a CPU two to three times
    slower.
  - The ×4 scores start from 81×61, an input the stage never sees, so they
    understate ×4 slightly; the side-by-side on real frames agreed.
