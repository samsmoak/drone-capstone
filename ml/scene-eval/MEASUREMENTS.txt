SCENE MEASUREMENTS — where scene@1's numbers come from
======================================================

scene@1 (backend/agent/cropwatcher/pipeline/stages/classify/scene.py) says
whether the camera's VIEW CHANGED while a reading departed. The camera cannot
see heat; this is the honest thing it can add. Change a constant → change its
line here, with the measurement that justifies it.

STATUS (2026-10-09): measured on the lab laptop's frames — 8 760 frames in 44
sessions, 5 936 with a position. The frames stay on that laptop — the
repository is public.

    cd backend/agent && .venv/bin/python ../../ml/scene-eval/evaluate.py \
        "<data folder>" [--sample 600]
