SENSOR-FAULT MEASUREMENTS — where every number in the cleaner comes from
=======================================================================

The cleaner (backend/agent/cropwatcher/pipeline/stages/clean/robust.py) cites
this file for each threshold. Change a threshold → change its line here, with
the measurement that justifies it.

STATUS (2026-10-09): robust@1 replaced hampel@1 (Kevin's, 2026-10-03), on the
owner's call, after scoring BOTH on the same 66 real flights with the same
planted faults (THE COMPARISON, below). Measured on 158 real flights recorded
on the lab laptop (88 590 readings, 2026-09-16 … 10-09), of which the 66 with
≥ 287 rows are long enough to plant into. The flights stay on that laptop —
the repository is public; only the pipeline fixture flight is committed.

  Re-run on any folder of flights:
    cd backend/agent && .venv/bin/python ../../ml/sensor-faults/evaluate.py \
        --quiet --cleaner robust --seeds 0-4 "<data folder>/flights"
    (--cleaner hampel scores hampel@1, read from the commit that last held it)

                         hampel@1            robust@1          target
  planted faults caught  17 621 / 18 095     18 095 / 18 095   ≥ 99.9 %
                         (97.38 %)           (100.00 %)
  false flags            1 567 / 1 117 266   119 / 1 117 266   ≤ 0.05 %

  flags inside the       632                 0                 0
  hand-warmer ramp
  time per flight        38.7 ms             19.5 ms           ≤ hampel's
  position and battery   not checked         945 / 945 caught

  Planted per flight (plant_faults.plant): a spike in raw_temp, corrected_temp
  and pressure; a missing raw_temp and pressure; an out-of-range raw_temp and
  pressure; 15 stuck raw_temp and pressure readings; one lost row and three
  lost rows; then a hand-warmer ramp (+5 °C over 10 s, held) on both
  temperatures — a REAL event, never to be flagged. plant_extras: a 1 m
  position jump where a base station was received and the estimate was
  steady; a 0 V battery reading; a battery jump of 1.1 V from the reading
  before.

  Scoring rules (evaluate.py): a gap the recording itself proves (a skipped
  index, lost time — 16 in the corpus) counts as real, not false; a raw_temp
  spike or stuck run is also one in corrected_temp, which is computed from it.


WHY hampel@1 FLAGGED WHAT IT DID  (measured before robust@1 was written)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Of hampel@1's 332 false flags on seed 0, 292 were corrected_temp "spikes",
  every one within 0.6 s of a thermal_state change, 278 of them with raw_temp
  clean. The correction engine scales the board's offset per state
  (telemetry/correction.py), so corrected_temp STEPS each time the state flips
  — and the state flips a median of 4 times a flight, up to 79, with 60 % of
  state stretches under 5 readings. A held step shorter than half the window
  is what a median calls a spike. The same steps inside the planted ramp made
  most of its ramp flags. Its 23 missed faults (seed 0) were all stuck runs
  (15 pressure, 4 raw_temp, 4 corrected_temp). It restarted its count at
  every gap; counting across gaps is the only change to robust@1's stuck
  check, and robust@1 misses none.

  → robust@1 judges corrected_temp by the OFFSET (raw − corrected) against
    neighbours in the SAME thermal state, inherits raw_temp's spike and stuck
    flags, and counts stuck runs across gaps.


TIMING  (gap)
─────────────
  Interval between readings     min 0.080 s · median 0.100 s · p99 0.104 s ·
                                max 0.120 s (the fixture flight)
  → EXPECTED_PERIOD_S = 0.1, GAP_S = 2.5 × 0.1 = 0.25 s: twice the worst
    interval seen.
  The row index is consecutive in the CSV: the recorder numbers each row as
  it builds it (telemetry/reader.py, rows_written). A skipped index is a lost
  row, and catches the single lost row that time alone cannot (0.2 s < 0.25 s).
  Real gaps in the corpus: 16 in 158 flights.


RANGE  (out_of_range)
─────────────────────
  Temperature and pressure come from the BMP388 (telemetry/correction.py).
  Bosch BMP388 datasheet, BST-BMP388-DS001, section 1 "Specification":
    pressure                    300 … 1250 hPa
    operating temperature       −40 … +85 °C
  → applied to raw_temp, corrected_temp (converted for °F flights) and
    station_pressure_hpa.
  Battery: a one-cell LiPo; the corpus ranges 2.79 … 4.19 V (2.79 V is sag
    under full thrust). → 2.5 … 4.4 V.
  Position: Lighthouse V2 reaches a few metres from its base station.
    → |x|, |y|, |z| ≤ 20 m.


MISSING
───────
  The recorder skips a row whose temperature, pressure, battery or position
  has not arrived (telemetry/reader.py, _build), so a missing value means the
  file was damaged or a NaN came off the sensor. The loader turns an empty
  cell into None and "nan" into NaN (pipeline/sources.py, _number): both are
  flagged.


SPIKE  (Hampel identifier)
──────────────────────────
  Over a rolling window of 11 readings (1.1 s), on the fixture flight:

                         scaled MAD (1.4826 × MAD)   largest |value − median|
                         median     5th pct   min
  raw_temp      °C       0.0378     0.0030    0.0015   0.0374
  pressure      hPa      0.0127     0.0054    0.0016   0.0443

  → WINDOW = 11, K = 3.
  → noise floor 0.05 °C (threshold ≥ 0.15 °C, four times the largest real
    deviation) and 0.03 hPa (threshold ≥ 0.09 hPa, twice it).

  THE 119 FLAGS THAT REMAIN "FALSE" (robust@1, all seeds) are not sensor
  faults and are flagged on purpose:
    114  pressure pulses of ~0.1 hPa lasting 2–3 readings, IN FLIGHT, at
         full-thrust bursts (thrust jumping to 65 535, mostly at takeoff): the
         propellers' own pressure, not the room's. Both cleaners flag them;
         the classifier must not read them as the room.
      5  pressure held to the last digit for 10+ readings — the same 5 in
         hampel@1.
  They stay counted as false above so the comparison is like for like.


OFFSET  (corrected_temp)
────────────────────────
  Between two readings in the SAME thermal_state, the engine's offset
  (raw − corrected) moves at most 0.076 °C (p99.9 0.040 °C; 158 flights). It
  jumps when the state changes — by design.
  → OFFSET_FLOOR_C = 0.05 °C: threshold ≥ 0.15 °C, twice the largest real
    in-state step. MIN_SAME_STATE = 2 neighbours to judge it at all.


STUCK
─────
  Longest run of exactly identical consecutive values: raw_temp 2,
  station_pressure_hpa 2 (fixture); battery_v 28 (logged at a slower rate).
  → STUCK_SAMPLES = 10 (1 s), five times the longest real run, on raw_temp and
    station_pressure_hpa only, counted across gaps. corrected_temp inherits
    raw_temp's. battery_v is never checked for stuck.


POSITION  (untrusted, implausible)
──────────────────────────────────
  lighthouse_received is 0 for every row of the median flight in the corpus
  (the older flights never received a base station). A position with no
  station received is the IMU coasting → untrusted, all three axes.
  With a station received, the step speed between readings: p50 0.007 m/s,
  p99 7.5 m/s — the estimate does jump (the agent's own log warns "a position
  that jumped 6 cm in 22 ms … check the base-station geometry").
  → implausible above MAX_PLAUSIBLE_SPEED_M_S = 2.0 m/s, the flight guard's
    own number (safety/flight_guard.py — imported, not copied), against the
    last good position no more than 0.5 s back (PLAUSIBLE_HORIZON_S); after
    that the estimate that stayed somewhere new is believed.
  On the real flights robust@1 marks about 20 readings a flight implausible
  (6 820 over 66 flights × 5 seeds): real estimator jumps.


BATTERY  (implausible)
──────────────────────
  Largest step between two readings: 0.78 V (p99.9 0.36 V) — sag under a
  throttle step. → BATTERY_STEP_V = 1.0 V, against the last good reading no
  more than 0.5 s back.
