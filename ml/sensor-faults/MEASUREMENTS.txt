TIMING  (gap)
─────────────
  Interval between readings     min 0.080 s · median 0.100 s · p99 0.104 s ·
                                max 0.120 s
  → EXPECTED_PERIOD_S = 0.1, GAP_S = 2.5 × 0.1 = 0.25 s: twice the worst
    interval seen.
  The row index is consecutive in the CSV: the recorder numbers each row as
  it builds it (telemetry/reader.py, rows_written). A skipped index is a lost
  row, and catches the single lost row that time alone cannot (0.2 s < 0.25 s).


RANGE  (out_of_range)
─────────────────────
  Temperature and pressure come from the BMP388 (telemetry/correction.py).
  Bosch BMP388 datasheet, BST-BMP388-DS001, section 1 "Specification":
    pressure                    300 … 1250 hPa
    operating temperature       −40 … +85 °C
  → applied to raw_temp, corrected_temp (converted for °F flights) and
    station_pressure_hpa. CHECK THESE AGAINST THE PDF: it is not in
    docs/hardware/, so they were taken from the published datasheet, not the
    repo.
  Seen in the fixture: raw_temp 29.9 … 34.2 °C (the board runs warm),
  corrected_temp 20.5 … 26.7 °C, pressure 1026 … 1027 hPa.


MISSING
───────
  0 missing values in all 400 rows. The recorder skips a row whose
  temperature, pressure, battery or position has not arrived
  (telemetry/reader.py, _build), so a missing value means the file was damaged
  or a NaN came off the sensor. The loader turns an empty cell into None and
  "nan" into NaN (pipeline/sources.py, _number): both are flagged.


SPIKE  (Hampel, PROVISIONAL)
────────────────────────────
  Over a rolling window of 11 readings (1.1 s), on the fixture:

                         scaled MAD (1.4826 × MAD)   largest |value − median|
                         median     5th pct   min
  raw_temp      °C       0.0378     0.0030    0.0015   0.0374
  corrected     °C       0.0424     0.0018    0.0005   0.0090
  pressure      hPa      0.0127     0.0054    0.0016   0.0443

  The scaled MAD falls near zero on quiet stretches, so without a floor the
  threshold would too, and ordinary noise would be called a spike.
  → WINDOW = 11, K = 3.
  → noise floor 0.05 °C for both temperatures: threshold ≥ 0.15 °C, four times
    the largest real deviation (0.037 °C).
  → noise floor 0.03 hPa for pressure: threshold ≥ 0.09 hPa, twice the
    largest real deviation (0.044 hPa).

  Largest real step from one reading to the next: raw_temp 0.055 °C,
  pressure 0.050 hPa, corrected_temp 4.64 °C. That last one is NOT noise: at
  row 27 thermal_state goes IDLE → FLIGHT (COOLING) as the drone takes off,
  and the correction engine moves corrected_temp from 22.01 to 26.65 °C and
  holds it, while raw_temp stays flat at 34.22 °C. It is why the cleaner has
  no rate-of-change rule: a held step moves the median with it.
