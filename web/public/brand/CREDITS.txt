(https://unsplash.com/license — commercial use, no permission or attribution
required). Credited here anyway so it can be traced and replaced:

  unsplash photo-1597353361282-e3d3af9f4ab7  (Rythik, PAf9rj_DKCU)
  "Gray and black drone with white background"

  drone-hero.webp      the whole photograph, background flattened to white
  drone-mark-256.png   the drone on a rounded white tile — the logo beside the
                       wordmark in the website and the desktop app
  icon-512.png         the same tile, for the web manifest
  maskable-512.png     full-bleed with a safe margin, for OS-masked icons

The website's app/icon.png, app/apple-icon.png and app/favicon.ico, and the
desktop app's src-tauri/icons/, are generated from the same photograph.
